The Self

Whether the universe is deterministic or non-deterministic, the individual can be perceived as a container of consciousness whose knowledge and belief both of themself and their environment is informed by a memory of a history of choices made and paths taken. This notion of the individual is not equivalent to the notion of self. Imagine a set of pathways along which the state of the universe and the individual within it progresses. Imagine that the individual along a path had the ability to glimpse the conscious experience of the individual along a different path. It seems a notion of the self would emerge as the qualitative expression of one such path as being more true to the notion of 'self' than another.

We can imagine an individual with a belief as to what the self is that contradicts the same belief held by the same individual were they to glimpse these alternate pathways. Were the individual to dissolve this notion of self, they would enter a drift state across aforementioned pathways, perceiving versions of the individual that affirm or contradict the broader notion of what the self is. As the notion of self re-emerges, the individual would ritualize actions that bound the individual to that notion of self, until further contradiction is perceived, at which point the cycle repeats itself. The conscious experience of one such cycle is as follows. 


    There is a vision of a self that occupies a space.
    There is a belief that this is the self. 
    There are rituals that affirm this belief.
    The self occupies this space.
